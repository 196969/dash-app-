Business Plan — working document

Ten parts, worked in order. Every section carries fields to complete, a check-off that closes it, and a named blank where no record answers yet.

How this document works

  • Work it in order: the model in Part 7 reads what you fill in Parts 2 to 6.
  • Fill the underlined fields; each carries a hint saying what good looks like.
  • Tick the check-offs. A part closes when every box is ticked, not when the pages are full.
  • Leave the blanks visible. A red panel names the field that would light it.
Measured — the figure comes from a record, and the record is named beside it.
Stated position — a judgement we hold and defend as a judgement, never as fact.
Blind — no record answers it. The field that would light it is named. Nothing is estimated into the gap.

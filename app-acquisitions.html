The problem

Most owners discover what their company is worth during diligence, from a buyer, under time pressure, with no leverage left. The value was decided years earlier by records nobody kept, a concentration nobody reduced and a dependency nobody replaced.

The governing principle

Decision support — never a valuation, fairness, legal or tax opinion. A valuation produced by software is a position, labelled as one.

One platform, two workflows

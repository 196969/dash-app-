Brought in from Dash Brain

What needs you, read from what the book holds.

What it does

One ranked list of what needs your attention, assembled from the records Dash already keeps: month-end close steps not yet done, unpaid payables, covenant breaches, compliance dates, silent deals, open commitments and open contradictions. Every row names the store it came from and the figure it read. A store with nothing in it reads as a measured zero; a store that was never written reads Blind, and says so rather than disappearing.

The rule, stated

A dated deadline within 14 days, or already past, needs you. A later dated deadline can wait. An item with no date this screen can read is undated, and is never sorted into either. The rule renders above the list with the date it was evaluated against.

The morning read

A short brief built from the same list when you open Dash Brain: what needs a decision today, what can wait, what went quiet. It is dated with the moment it was generated. It is not sent, and cannot be: Dash has no scheduler and no delivery. It is ready when you arrive.

The architectural statement

Brain senses. Second Seat acts. Brain writes nothing to the books. Every write to Second Seat’s records passes through its one writer, under the gate, stamped with the acting seat. One write sits outside Second Seat: The read’s own capture, which writes Brain’s history and an audit row directly — stamped by the pipeline, touching no book. As of Pass 0, a seat whose id is not on the register is no seat: every gated action refuses by name, and nothing falls back to the Owner.

Senses

Brain reads; it does not act. Nine signals, each read from a view-model the firm already trusts and recomputed from nothing: the trial balance tie, cash against the next thirty days’ obligations, client concentration, weighted pipeline against plan, sequences overdue, decide-by dates passed, unmeasured tiles older than thirty days, rework share, and runway in months. Each names its source, its threshold, the principle behind the threshold, and its as-of. A signal whose record does not exist reads unmeasured with the record named, never zero; a rate from fewer than ten events reads unmeasured with the count shown. On every Dash Refresh and on each period close, Brain reads every signal and appends one capture to its own history — the one named exception — and writes nothing else. A tripped signal produces a proposal: a stamped sentence in Brain’s history, owner-less until a seat claims it under the gate. Brain is a complete read the moment you open it, and states the age of everything it shows. Reached in Dash from the sidebar, under Work, as Brain: what needs you.

Needs you → Delegate

A row in Needs you becomes a delegation in one click, carrying its source: the title from the row, the measure from the rule that put it there, the decide-by date from the row’s own date, the plan from its route and store — nothing invented, every field traceable. The delegation records where it came from, and the Seat log shows it. A completed, handed-over delegation marks its row satisfied, naming the delegation; a row already carrying an open delegation names it rather than permitting a second; a row whose store is Blind refuses, naming the store. The refusal ladder on Delegate says, per field, what the book would need and where it would be written. A Senses proposal is claimed the same way: Delegate is pre-filled from the capture and the delegation is written under the gate by the seat that claims it; Senses reads who claimed it from Second Seat’s records. Brain senses, Second Seat acts. Before a delegation is confirmed, Delegate reads five things from its lineage: the blast radius (the store, the screens that display it, the other Needs-you rows on the same store), the cost of waiting (measured from the store, or Blind naming it), the precedent (a prior delegation on the same store with its outcome, else no precedent recorded), the council’s read on the subject (the lenses bound to the store by their declared reads, each with its weight and stance), and the dissent named (the Governing seats with their grounds, never averaged). Every delegation carries that read; Correspondence carries a frame through from its lineage, the recipient from the row’s owner and refused where there is none.

The verdict, read by every hub

One cross-domain line, computed in Brain from the signal register, once: how many signals tripped and which, how many are unmeasured and which, the confidence as measured-of-nine, the date, and the sources. The fourteen other hubs display it as read from Brain and say so; none recomputes it, and the proof is that a change to Brain’s input moves every hub by the same text. Beside it sit the disagreement surface — where an edition’s own read differs from Brain’s on the same store, both shown, neither averaged — and the declaration ledger: every edition, what it declares where a program can read it, satisfied or Blind, and the date declared. Four of fifteen editions declare; no declaration carries a date.

Beside the verdict

The one number earned is the verdict’s confidence, measured of nine, shown with its formula, every input, every source and store, and its limitation: it counts signals, not evidence, and an unmeasured signal is kept in the denominator and never counted. The change ledger reads Brain’s own history — the latest two captures, signal by signal, from, to, when and source — and reads Blind until two captures exist. The contradiction census counts Brain’s own read by class and the Acquisitions register by status. The silence read names every store no runtime declaration reads. The instrument self-report states what each of Brain’s instruments last did and what it cannot do. The council’s read on the verdict names the lenses bound to the stores Brain reads, their weight and stance, and the Governing seats with their grounds; nothing is averaged. The DRA 2026 Core Standard’s Founders72 review comes first in every build and its criteria have not been supplied; that gap is stated on screen, not filled.

The attention budget

The Owner declares one whole number in Tune the Seat. On Needs you the first that-many rows, in the order the rule already gives them, sit above a drawn line; every other row stays on screen below it, held, with its rank, the reason it is held and the threshold that would raise it — the date it would need, or how many rows above it must close; undated rows show the rank to reach only; rows that could not be read, or had nothing to rank, read Blind for threshold with the reason. Nothing is hidden, removed or re-ranked, and the count is stated on every render: above, held, in all. With no budget declared, no line is drawn and every row is shown as before. Brain reads the budget and never sets it.

The learning loop

Brain reads its own record against what happened next. One loop is measurable today: a proposal in a sense capture paired to the delegation a seat claimed from it, by the proposal’s signal and the capture’s moment — two written records, never a guess; a delegation naming a capture Brain does not hold reads unpaired. The second loop, a watch that fired and was later contradicted, is deferred: a firing records no threshold, and no writer change is authorised. The third, predictions, is deferred: Brain records none. So the calibration score reads Blind by construction, with its reason. Uptake — the share of proposals a seat claimed — shows beside it, labelled as uptake, and reads Blind below ten proposals.

Proprioception

On every Brain screen, one line says what Brain can feel of itself, recomputed on every render: how many of the fifteen editions declare what they read, and how many of Brain’s own nine signals are satisfied; how many of the declared stores are stale beyond thirty days, fresh, or Blind; how long since Brain last captured; and what it has never been able to see. Senses carries the full read. Ages come only from record dates that carry a year — no year is inferred, and deadlines and milestones are plans, not records. Brain’s own declarations count as satisfied when their signal is measured, a correction to the C1 ledger, stated on screen. Nothing is written.

What it writes

Per screen. Of the Brain screens, four write nothing: Needs you, Contradictions, Memory and the Thinking wheel. The fifth, The read, carries one owner-initiated capture action that writes Brain’s own history, audited and undoable. Of the Second Seat section, eleven screens write nothing: the hub, Seat Home, Ask, Everywhere, Call a Seat, the Seat log, Visuals, Correspondence, Toolkit, Architecture and Boundary. Eight write, each under connectors.ssx and each only through an action you click, with its own audit row stamped by Dash with the acting seat and role: Standing Work (arming a watch, Owner only; recording a firing, an escalation or a stand-down), Delegate (opening a delegation), Handover (saving a handover, completing a delegation), House Format (recording each production, through the existing report path) Tune the Seat (a standing instruction, a vocabulary entry, a never-touch item or the signature block, Owner only; every entry dated and seat-stamped), The pack (declaring a piece, Owner only; producing it through the existing report path) Philosophy (declaring purpose, vision, choice or intent, scoring the eight disciplines, recording a dialogue; Owner only, every entry dated and seat-stamped) and Enrichments (declaring, signing or dissenting on a door; scoring a pre-mortem; declaring the evidence half-life; logging hours; costing or pre-committing a walk-away on a delegation; Owner or Operator). No ledger writer changes: account 1000 keeps its single writer and period close stays Owner-only. Nothing is written when a screen is opened.

What it will not do

It cannot send, pay or delete, so there is nothing to withhold and no confirmation to ask for. It does not run while Dash is closed, so it cannot hand off work that keeps going or flag silence as it happens, only when you open it. It uses no model: every row is read from a store and every rank follows the rule above.

Second Seat’s limits, stated

Standing Work is evaluated when you open it: a report with a threshold, not a watch that runs while Dash is closed. Delegate holds the plan and its refusals; execution is not built. House Format produces through the existing report path, and states on screen which elements of the DRA format that path does not produce (a thin page border, page numbers, the monogram in documents). Model and Pages route to their owners, the Scenario planner and the Client snapshot; Meetings, Seats and Trust and Right Judgement route to the Meeting cadence, Seats & roles with Audit by seat, and the council. Voice carries no route: Ask Dash speaks its reads and displays no store as text; reach Bella from the Talk to Dash control on every screen. Correspondence is an assembled frame — thread history, live links and the signature declared on Tune the Seat — that writes no prose and makes no generative call; the drafting itself is not built. Build and Find are not built: each needs a model call Dash does not make. Ask takes structured questions only. The document pack is a register the Owner declares, piece by piece with the store paths it reads; the application holds no pack piece of its own. The Toolkit is folded in by reference to the Operating Toolkit’s own definition, never copied. The philosophy layer reads its standing instructions from Tune the Seat so the two cannot drift. The nine enrichments are built in the house form, each on its own record and each stating what would make it fire. The blast-radius read is measured, not claimed: with one seat on the register, every writer in Dash stops if that seat stops; the count moves on the register, not in the software. Second Seat is complete across its four passes; In the Tools has no pass in the Master Brief.

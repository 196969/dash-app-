The problem

High-stakes decisions still begin with fragmented evidence. Deal teams reconcile data rooms, financial systems, contracts, interviews and advisor reports before capital commits. New leaders inherit incomplete KPIs, inconsistent records, broken processes and the urgency to produce immediately. The shared cost: slow diagnosis, duplicated work, missed contradictions, expensive expertise, and decisions that depend on who happens to be in the room.

The governing principle

Decision support — not autonomous investment advice or audit assurance. Every finding links to its source, states its uncertainty, and passes a human gate before it becomes a recommendation.

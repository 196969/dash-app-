## The standing sweep (Item 485, 27 Nov 2026)
- `python3 sweep/sweep.py --control` first: it plants a missing link and a thrown error in a copy, and must catch both.
- `python3 sweep/sweep.py` before every delivery that touches the CEO area or a standalone: every standalone clicked, every link target checked, every download opened, all 54 CEO screens exported to Excel and opened, and the CEO pack opened. Exit 1 means do not ship.
- `.github/workflows/sweep.yml` runs both on every push to `branch`. A red run is a fault to fix, not a warning.
- The live read-back after a push still applies: the sweep proves the build, the read-back proves what is served.

Executive Interview Readiness Program

Who it serves
Senior leaders in an executive search who need every conversation with an employer to land: directors, vice-presidents and C-level candidates moving into a new role.
The promise
Walk into every interview with a package the hiring team can hold, and a brief the client can rehearse from, so nothing is improvised.
What makes it different
Everything is drawn from one locked career record, every piece is built per role rather than reused, and the readiness score shows the client exactly what is done and what is not.

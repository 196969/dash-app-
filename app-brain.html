Dash Brain

What needs you, read from what the book holds.
Open Dash →

What it does

One ranked list of what needs your attention, assembled from the records Dash already keeps: month-end close steps not yet done, unpaid payables, covenant breaches, compliance dates, silent deals, open commitments and open contradictions. Every row names the store it came from and the figure it read. A store with nothing in it reads as a measured zero; a store that was never written reads Blind, and says so rather than disappearing.

The rule, stated

A dated deadline within 14 days, or already past, needs you. A later dated deadline can wait. An item with no date this screen can read is undated, and is never sorted into either. The rule renders above the list with the date it was evaluated against.

The morning read

A short brief built from the same list when you open Dash Brain: what needs a decision today, what can wait, what went quiet. It is dated with the moment it was generated. It is not sent, and cannot be: Dash has no scheduler and no delivery. It is ready when you arrive.

The architectural statement

Brain senses. Second Seat acts. Brain writes nothing to the books. Every write to Second Seat’s records passes through its one writer, under the gate, stamped with the acting seat. One write sits outside Second Seat: The read’s own capture, which writes Brain’s history and an audit row directly — stamped by the pipeline, touching no book. As of Pass 0, a seat whose id is not on the register is no seat: every gated action refuses by name, and nothing falls back to the Owner.

Senses

Brain reads; it does not act. Nine signals, each read from a view-model the firm already trusts and recomputed from nothing: the trial balance tie, cash against the next thirty days’ obligations, client concentration, weighted pipeline against plan, sequences overdue, decide-by dates passed, unmeasured tiles older than thirty days, rework share, and runway in months. Each names its source, its threshold, the principle behind the threshold, and its as-of. A signal whose record does not exist reads unmeasured with the record named, never zero; a rate from fewer than ten events reads unmeasured with the count shown. On every Dash Refresh and on each period close, Brain reads every signal and appends one capture to its own history — the one named exception — and writes nothing else. A tripped signal produces a proposal: a stamped sentence in Brain’s history, owner-less until a seat claims it under the gate. Brain is a complete read the moment you open it, and states the age of everything it shows. Reached from the Apps launcher as Dash Brain · Senses.

What it writes

Per screen. Of the Brain screens, four write nothing: Needs you, Contradictions, Memory and the Thinking wheel. The fifth, The read, carries one owner-initiated capture action that writes Brain’s own history, audited and undoable. Of the Second Seat section, eleven screens write nothing: the hub, Seat Home, Ask, Everywhere, Call a Seat, the Seat log, Visuals, Correspondence, Toolkit, Architecture and Boundary. Eight write, each under connectors.ssx and each only through an action you click, with its own audit row stamped by Dash with the acting seat and role: Standing Work (arming a watch, Owner only; recording a firing, an escalation or a stand-down), Delegate (opening a delegation), Handover (saving a handover, completing a delegation), House Format (recording each production, through the existing report path) Tune the Seat (a standing instruction, a vocabulary entry, a never-touch item or the signature block, Owner only; every entry dated and seat-stamped), The pack (declaring a piece, Owner only; producing it through the existing report path) Philosophy (declaring purpose, vision, choice or intent, scoring the eight disciplines, recording a dialogue; Owner only, every entry dated and seat-stamped) and Enrichments (declaring, signing or dissenting on a door; scoring a pre-mortem; declaring the evidence half-life; logging hours; costing or pre-committing a walk-away on a delegation; Owner or Operator). No ledger writer changes: account 1000 keeps its single writer and period close stays Owner-only. Nothing is written when a screen is opened.

What it will not do

It cannot send, pay or delete, so there is nothing to withhold and no confirmation to ask for. It does not run while Dash is closed, so it cannot hand off work that keeps going or flag silence as it happens, only when you open it. It uses no model: every row is read from a store and every rank follows the rule above.

Its screens

Needs you (the hub) · The read · Contradictions · Memory · Thinking wheel · and the Second Seat section: Hub · Seat Home · Ask · Everywhere · Standing Work · Delegate · Call a Seat · Handover · Seat log · House Format · Visuals · Tune the Seat · Correspondence · The pack · Toolkit · Philosophy · Enrichments · Architecture · Boundary. Second Seat’s limits, stated

Standing Work is evaluated when you open it: a report with a threshold, not a watch that runs while Dash is closed. Delegate holds the plan and its refusals; execution is not built. House Format produces through the existing report path, and states on screen which elements of the DRA format that path does not produce (a thin page border, page numbers, the monogram in documents). Model and Pages route to their owners, the Scenario planner and the Client snapshot; Meetings, Seats and Trust and Right Judgement route to the Meeting cadence, Seats & roles with Audit by seat, and the council. Voice carries no route: Ask Dash speaks its reads and displays no store as text; reach Bella from the Talk to Dash control on every screen. Correspondence is an assembled frame — thread history, live links and the signature declared on Tune the Seat — that writes no prose and makes no generative call; the drafting itself is not built. Build and Find are not built: each needs a model call Dash does not make. Ask takes structured questions only. The document pack is a register the Owner declares, piece by piece with the store paths it reads; the application holds no pack piece of its own. The Toolkit is folded in by reference to the Operating Toolkit’s own definition, never copied. The philosophy layer reads its standing instructions from Tune the Seat so the two cannot drift. The nine enrichments are built in the house form, each on its own record and each stating what would make it fire. The blast-radius read is measured, not claimed: with one seat on the register, every writer in Dash stops if that seat stops; the count moves on the register, not in the software. Second Seat is complete across its four passes; In the Tools has no pass in the Master Brief.
